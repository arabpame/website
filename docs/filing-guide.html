How to file a report

Four steps. Ninety seconds. One permanent case number.

Open the Report page on your phone. Each step checks itself before the next one opens, and tells you in plain words if something is missing.

earthlinkph.vercel.app/report
STEP 1 OF 4

What you saw

  • Tap one of the seven categories: Water, Forest, Waste, Air, Biodiversity, Land, Hazard.
  • Give it a short title and describe what is happening and since when.
  • Enter the date you saw it. Approximate is fine.
  • Choose the urgency. Each level says what it means.
Say to the roomThe category decides which government office the case is routed to.
STEP 2 OF 4

Where it is

  • Type the city or municipality and the province. Barangay and landmark are optional.
  • For a Metro Manila city, write Metro Manila as the province.
  • "Use my current location" works when you are standing at the site. Not at the venue.
Say to the roomThe platform knows every city and municipality in the country and places the pin itself.
STEP 3 OF 4

Evidence

  • Tap "Tap to add photographs" and pick two or three from your gallery. Up to ten.
  • Each shows a preview and its size. Photographs are optional.
Say to the roomThey are resized on the phone before sending, so it works on mobile data.
STEP 4 OF 4

About you

  • Name and contact are optional, or tick file anonymously.
  • Tick the consent box. This one is required.
  • Check the summary, then tap File this report. Two to five seconds.
Say to the roomAnonymous reports get a case number and a public page too. Personal details are never published.

Then it moves through six public stages

The same rail appears on every case page, so anyone can see how far along it is.

Reported
Filed. Nothing checked yet.
Under verification
Checked, duplicates merged.
Referred
Sent to the office that can act. Days waiting are counted.
In progress
Inspection, order, mission or clean-up under way.
Resolved
Fixed, and the result measured.
Monitoring
Watched so it does not return.

Before the day

  • Rehearse twice on your phone, on mobile data, not venue Wi-Fi.
  • Have two or three photographs of a real problem in your gallery. No faces.
  • Decide the city and province in advance and type them.
  • Have your Gmail open on the big screen so the room watches the email land.
  • Afterwards, delete the practice report so the register stays honest.

If something goes wrong

  • "Reporting is temporarily unavailable": a server setting is missing. Nothing is lost. Tell Erick.
  • "We could not find that city": check the spelling of both fields.
  • No email: the report is still saved and on the map. Check spam.
Reports are handled under the Data Privacy Act of 2012. Names and contacts are never published. EARTHLINK PH · SEPTEMBER 2026
